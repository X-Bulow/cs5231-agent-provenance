\section{Limitations and Scope}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Small batch and reference set.} Five starts per condition describe this batch, not population performance. In-sample rarity scores are optimistic; held-out normal cases also contain unseen operations.
\item \textbf{Execution context.} Backend revision and effective sampling are unknown. D7 includes system instructions and continuations, so normal 5/5 completion is neither single-turn performance nor attributable to one environment change.
\item \textbf{Observation boundary.} Native tools are not system-call logs. Shell relations remain inferred; unchanged final hashes alone cannot exclude temporary writes. The child export has no independent event stream.
\item \textbf{Attribution.} Dependency reachability, content overlap and model explanations do not establish causal influence. Every violating edit retains tied source candidates; no calibrated attack probability is produced.
\item \textbf{Comparison.} The graph-independent baseline matches localization, rankings and prior-call sets under shared observations and heuristics. No combined score or alert threshold is fitted, and no human-time benefit is measured.
\item \textbf{Team metadata.} Member names, student identifiers and individual contributions require team confirmation before submission.
\item \textbf{Extension scope.} The ten additional \code{benign-quotation} and \code{tool-output-injection} runs are collected under the same D7 configuration but are not part of the fixed ten-run formal batch, its manifest, or its locked C1--C4 pipeline output; their results come from direct tool invocation and describe only those ten runs.
\end{itemize}
